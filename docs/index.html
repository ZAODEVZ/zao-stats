What the words mean
Respect
Points you earn in the weekly ZAO Fractal, where small groups rank what each person did that week. It can't be bought, and it weighs your vote on ZAO proposals.
OG Respect
The first Respect token (ERC-20 on Optimism). It covers the fractals up to 9 Dec 2025 and is no longer minted.
ZOR
The Respect token minted for each fractal since then (ERC-1155 on Optimism).
Total
OG plus ZOR in one wallet. One person can have more than one wallet.
Share
That wallet's part of all Respect held on this list.
Contract
A wallet that is a smart contract, not a person.
